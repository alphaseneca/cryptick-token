Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need to be technical to use this?
No! Cryptick works automatically with Google, Apple, GitHub, Microsoft, and 1Password. Just plug it in or connect via Bluetooth, tap your finger, and you are signed in.
What happens if I lose my key?
You can register a backup key (like our Duo pack) or use your account recovery codes. And because Cryptick requires your physical fingerprint, anyone who finds your lost key cannot unlock your accounts.
Can someone steal my fingerprint from the key?
Never. Your fingerprint template is verified entirely within offline secure hardware silicon. It is never uploaded to the internet or accessible to your computer.
Does it work on my phone?
Yes. Cryptick connects wirelessly to iPhones, iPads, and Android devices using standard Bluetooth Low Energy, as well as plugging directly into USB-C ports.
